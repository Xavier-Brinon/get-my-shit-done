** Objective

Write retroactive verification reports for Phases 1 and 2, then promote
their 4 partial requirements to satisfied status in REQUIREMENTS.org.
This closes the procedural verification gap identified by the v1.0
milestone audit --- these phases predate the verification workflow step
but have substantive evidence of completion in their SUMMARY files and
committed artifacts.

GAPS-01 as "partial" because VERIFICATION.org files were missing despite
SUMMARY evidence confirming completion. This plan produces the missing
verification artifacts so the audit trail is complete.

*Output:* Two VERIFICATION.org files and an updated REQUIREMENTS.org.

workflow step (verification) to phases that were executed before the step
existed. The verification reports follow the same format as
=03-VERIFICATION.org= (the existing exemplar) but include a
=:re_verification: true= flag to mark them as retroactive.
